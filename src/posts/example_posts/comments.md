This is a fork of [Kate Bagenzo](https://katebagenzo.neocities.org/)'s [Strawberry Starter](https://strawberrystarter.neocities.org/) blog template that adds the ability to easily embed external comments onto pages and blog posts. These comments are built to work on static websites and hosts such as [Neocities](https://neocities.org/).

This can be done by embedding interactions from posts on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/), or by using the [CommentBox](https://commentbox.io/) service. There is also space to copy and paste HTML from other comment box providers. You can enable comments on a post by post basis. You can even enable comments on any page. You can choose what kind of comments to enable, or enable all of them at once.

## First, some important information

These comments rely on external resources and hosts, and by using them you take on any risk involved. Comments are stored elsewhere and referenced into your site when its loaded. If any of these hosts go down, the comments won't load in, if the data is deleted, so will the comments. The comments are not stored on your site's folder, nor on your host's servers, backing up your site won't back up your comments. You should enable comments knowing these risks, and back up or archive them through each service.

### For those curious about what services are involved

- The Bluesky comments rely on **the Bluesky network and their services**. It is the intention of the Bluesky developers to make their network resilient and independent of any one host, but as of writing most all of the network and its relays are hosted on Bluesky-owned infrastructure, with ways to self-host still limited, in-development and incomplete.
- The Bluesky comments also rely on **Github** services, and in particular **[Kayin's github-pages repository](https://github.com/Kayinnasaki/comment-embed)**. The github page is used to fetch the comments and work around restrictions imposed by Neocities. If Github or Kayin's account and repositories go down, so will the ability to retrieve Bluesky posts.
  - This is, not as much of an issue. The repository can easily be cloned and then rebuilt and hosted elsewhere by anyone. If you host your site outside of Neocities, its likely this dependency isn't necessary, and the template could be updated to work without it.
